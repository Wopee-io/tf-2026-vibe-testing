# 16:15 · Wrap-up & Q&A

**Time:** 16:15–17:00 · 45 min

**Goal:** winners, an honest debrief, and a plan for Monday.

## Steps

1. **Winners.** The votes from the Battle are counted.
2. **Speed gap, revisited.** Look at your two sticky notes from the
   [kick-off](00-kickoff.md). With today's stack, what would your QA number be now?
3. **Honest debrief.** Three questions:
   - What held you back? Tools, setup, the app, the team?
   - What surprised you? Which exhibit changed your mind?
   - What breaks in a real codebase? Where does today's approach stop scaling?
4. **Monday morning.** Pen and paper, 3 minutes, on your own. Write:
   - **Do Monday:** one thing you will try on Monday. Small enough to start before lunch.
   - **Takes longer:** one thing that needs your team. A tool, a process, a budget — who do you
     have to convince?
   - **Drop now:** one habit you stop. The one that today made look slow.

   Then share it: read it to your neighbour now, and take it to your team on Monday.
5. Questions.

## Where files go

Your work stays in your fork and your pull request. Keep them after today.

## Done when

You have your three Monday lines on paper.
